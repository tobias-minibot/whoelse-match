## Catalog design

> Since it is language apparently available to every kind of user, and applicable to every kind of question, we use "who else?" questions as a design for the intent catalog. Initially we provide it for the **200 most common request types** in human language.

## Human-language examples (verbatim)

- "Apartment who else?"
- "Delivery who else?"
- "Date who else?"
- "Voice AI, find me a ride-share"

## Example catalog implementation (co-working speaker)
